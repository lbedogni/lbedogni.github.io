IEEE Percom 2025 just ended. The conference was held in Washington D.C., USA. The conference brought together several academic and industry researchers from all around the world.

Together with Prof. Stefano Ferretti from the University of Bologna, Prof. Luca Bedogni ran the TRUSTSENSE workshop, which featured a keynote speech from Prof. Daniela Nicklas from the Univeristy of Bamberg (Germany), and 6 scientific presentations.
