# [2607.09295] Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning for SLA-Aware Network Slicing in UAV-Enabled MEC

**Field:** Edge Computing / UAV / Multi-Agent RL
**Date:** July 2026
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09295

## Summary
Proposes a predictive Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning (MARL) framework to maintain Service-Level Agreement (SLA) stability in UAV-enabled Mobile Edge Computing (MEC). It handles dynamic user mobility and stochastic task arrivals.

Key components:
- **Prediction Module:** Forecasts near-future user mobility to anticipate congestion.
- **SLA-Aware Reward:** Penalizes violation probability, duration, and energy consumption.
- **Algorithm:** Uses Multi-Agent Proximal Policy Optimization (MAPPO) with centralized training and decentralized execution.

## Key Results
- Significant improvement in SLA stability compared to baselines.
- Maintains competitive energy efficiency and delay performance.
- Approaches oracle-level performance with accurate predictive data.
