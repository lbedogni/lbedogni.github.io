# New Paper: LVMScissor: Split and Schedule Large Vision Model Inference on Mobile Edges via Salp Swarm Algorithm

**Summary:**
This article proposes 'LVMScissor', a framework for splitting and scheduling Large Vision Model (LVM) inference specifically for mobile edge environments. It utilizes the Salp Swarm Algorithm to optimize the split points and scheduling, addressing the latency challenges of running large models on resource-constrained devices.

**Key Findings/Results:**
- Leverages the Salp Swarm Algorithm to optimize inference latency in mobile edge computing.
- Addresses the complexity of managing Large Vision Models (LVMs) by partitioning them across edge devices.
- Provides a dynamic approach to scheduling that adapts to resource constraints at the edge.

**Relevance:**
Highly relevant to your work in split computing and edge inference, particularly for vision-based AI at the edge.
