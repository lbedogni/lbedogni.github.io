# Speculation at a Distance: Where Edge-Cloud Speculative Decoding Actually Pays Off

**Status:** To Read
**Date Found:** 2026-07-01
**Field:** Distributed Edge Inference / Speculative Decoding / LLM

## Abstract
Speculative decoding (SD) accelerates LLM inference by $1.5$-$3$ times when the draft and target models are co-located. This has motivated a variety of edge-cloud architectures. This paper provides a critical analysis of "speculation at a distance," identifying the conditions and network parameters under which edge-cloud speculative decoding actually provides a performance gain.

## Initial Analysis
This is a "sanity check" paper. It's crucial for anyone implementing speculative decoding in distributed systems to understand the boundary conditions where the communication overhead outweighs the computation gains.
