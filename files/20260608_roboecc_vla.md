# RoboECC: Multi-Factor-Aware Edge-Cloud Collaborative Deployment for VLA Models

**Date:** 2026-06-08
**Field:** VLA Models, Edge-Cloud Collaboration, Embodied AI

## Summary
RoboECC proposes an Edge-Cloud Collaborative (ECC) deployment strategy for Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models. It considers multiple factors (latency, compute, bandwidth) to decide how to split and deploy the model across the edge and cloud.

## Analysis
This paper applies distributed inference to the domain of embodied AI (Robotics). The "split" here is between the local edge (robot/controller) and the cloud. It's a practical application of Distributed Edge Inference where the cost of a "bad split" (high latency) can lead to physical failure in robotics.
