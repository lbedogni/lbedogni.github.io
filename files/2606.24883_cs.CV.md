# BenchX: Benchmarking AI Models for Cancer Detection and Localization with Demographic and Protocol Biases
- **Source:** [arXiv:2606.24883](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24883)
- **Date:** 2026-06-23
- **Field:** cs.CV

## Summary
Artificial intelligence (AI) has achieved remarkable success in medical imaging, but it is widely recognized that these models often perform inconsistently across real-world clinical settings. Such inconsistencies occur when patient demographics and imaging protocols vary, for example, in detecting small tumors, analyzing scans from different contrast phases, or evaluating patients of different ages or sexes. To quantify these inconsistencies, we develop a large-scale, open benchmark of 85,355 CT scans that systematically evaluates 12 tumor-detection AI models across tumor size, location, patient subgroup, and imaging protocol.
