# TL++: Accuracy and Privacy Preserving Traversal Learning for Distributed Intelligent Systems

**Authors:** Erdenebileg Batbaatar, Young Yoon
**Date:** June 2026
**Topic:** Distributed Intelligent Systems, Split Learning, Federated Learning

## Summary
TL++ introduces a traversal learning framework designed for distributed intelligent systems. It aims to bridge the gap between Federated Learning (FL) and Split Learning (SL). While FL suffers from high communication costs due to full-model exchange and SL struggles with recovering centralized mini-batch gradient behavior and privacy leaks of activations, TL++ proposes a "traversal" approach to optimize these trade-offs.

## Analysis
This paper is highly relevant to the core of **Split Computing** and **Distributed Edge Inference**. The focus on recovering centralized gradient behavior in a split setting is a critical technical challenge. The privacy-preserving aspect is also key for real-world IoT deployments.
