# SWARM-LLM: Collaborative Inference for Edge-based Small Language Models

**Authors:** Mostafa Dahshan, Quazi Mamun, Tanmoy Debnath
**Date:** June 2026
**Topic:** SLMs, Edge Computing, Collaborative AI

## Summary
SWARM-LLM introduces a routing and collaboration layer that enables a "swarm" of Small Language Models (SLMs) deployed on various edge devices to coordinate their inference process. By routing requests and collaborating on the generation task, the system can achieve capabilities closer to larger models while maintaining the low latency and privacy of edge deployment.

## Analysis
This is a direct implementation of **Distributed Edge Inference**. The "swarm" concept is an interesting approach to resource orchestration in an IoT environment, moving away from a single-client-single-server model to a peer-to-peer collaborative model.
