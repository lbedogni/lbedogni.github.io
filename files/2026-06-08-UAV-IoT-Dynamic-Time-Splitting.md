# Deterministic versus Stochastic Optimization for Joint Path Planning and Dynamic Time Splitting in Multiple-UAV-Cached IoT Networks

**Date:** 2026-06-08
**Field:** IoT, Edge Computing, UAV Networks

## Abstract
The paper investigates the optimization of data communication and energy harvesting (EH) in IoT networks where UAVs act as relays. It employs the Dynamic Time Splitting (DTS) method to manage the transmission of information and power from the source to the UAVs. The UAVs utilize harvested energy for both passive (backscatter) and active communication to the destination.

## Analysis
This work is highly relevant to the resource-constrained nature of IoT. By jointly optimizing path planning and time splitting, it addresses the critical trade-off between energy autonomy (via EH) and communication throughput. The use of UAVs as cached relays aligns with the goals of reducing latency and extending the reach of edge inference services in dynamic environments.

## Key Takeaways
- Joint optimization of path planning and DTS is superior to isolated optimization.
- Backscatter communication significantly reduces the energy footprint of UAV relays.
- Effective for scenarios with intermittent connectivity and high energy constraints.
