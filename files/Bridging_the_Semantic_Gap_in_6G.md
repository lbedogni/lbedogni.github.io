# Bridging the Semantic Gap in 6G: Tiny Language Models Under the Latency-Accuracy-Size Trilemma

**Authors:** Arnav Mathur, Garima Mathur, Rahul Jashvantbhai Pandya
**Date:** July 3, 2026
**Topic:** 6G, Semantic Communication, TinyLMs

## Summary
This paper discusses the shift in 6G wireless networks toward semantic communication, where the goal is to transmit "meaning" rather than bits. It specifically examines the use of Tiny Language Models (TinyLMs) as semantic encoders, analyzing the trade-offs between latency, accuracy, and model size (the "Latency-Accuracy-Size Trilemma").

## Key Contribution
- Analysis of the trilemma in the context of semantic communication for 6G.
- Investigation of deep learning-based semantic encoders and their efficiency compared to large transformer models.

## Notes
- Relevant for: 6G infrastructure, AI-native networks, and model compression for edge deployment.
