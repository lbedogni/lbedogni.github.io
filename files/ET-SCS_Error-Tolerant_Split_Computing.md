# ET-SCS: Error-Tolerant Split Computing System for Internet of Things

**Status:** To Read
**Date Found:** 2026-06-27
**Source:** IEEE Xplore
**URL:** https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11456673

## Summary
Proposes an error-tolerant split computing system (ET-SCS) designed for the IoT. The system focuses on training the model to be resilient to transmission errors that occur in the intermediate data sent between the edge and the cloud.

## Key Contributions
- **Transmission Error Awareness**: Trains the model specifically to handle noise and errors in the split-point data.
- **Dynamic Filtering**: Implements automatic adjustment of filter coefficients to enhance robustness.

## Relevance to Luca's Research
Relevant to **Split Computing** and **IoT**, focusing on the reliability and robustness of the communication link, which is a critical bottleneck in distributed inference.
