# DSSD: Efficient Edge-Device LLM Deployment and Collaborative Inference via Distributed Split Speculative Decoding
**arXiv ID:** 2507.12000
**Date:** July 2025
**Conference:** ICML 2025

## Summary
DSSD addresses the challenges of deploying Large Language Models (LLMs) in device-edge systems, specifically focusing on the resource limitations of devices and the communication overhead of collaborative inference.

## Key Contributions
- **Distributed Split Speculative Decoding (DSSD):** A novel architecture that partitions the verification phase of speculative decoding between the device and the edge.
- **Communication Optimization:** Replaces the transmission of multiple vocabulary distributions (uplink) with a single downlink transmission, significantly reducing latency.
- **Performance:** Maintains inference quality while outperforming existing collaborative frameworks in terms of latency and communication cost.

## Analysis & Relevance
This paper is highly relevant to Luca's interests in **Split Computing** and **Edge Computing**. It provides a concrete implementation of split-inference for LLMs, optimizing the communication bottleneck which is a central theme in distributed edge inference.
