# DSSD: Efficient Edge-Device Deployment and Collaborative Inference via Distributed Split Speculative Decoding

- **URL**: https://icml.cc/virtual/2025/47046
- **Date**: 2025 (ICML)
- **Key Concept**: **Distributed Split Speculative Decoding (DSSD)** for LLMs.
- **Analysis**:
    - Optimizes the collaboration between Small Language Models (SLMs) on-device and Large Language Models (LLMs) at the edge.
    - Innovation: Partitions the **verification phase** of speculative decoding between device and edge.
    - Impact: Reduces uplink communication costs by replacing multiple vocabulary distribution transmissions with a single downlink transmission.
    - Crucial for deploying LLMs in resource-constrained edge-cloud ecosystems.
