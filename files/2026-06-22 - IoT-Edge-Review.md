# A Comprehensive Review on IoT and Edge Computing in Electronics: Trends, Challenges, and Future Directions

**Source:** Journal of Electronic Design Technology, 2026
**Date:** March 18, 2026
**Topic:** IoT, Edge Computing, Electronics

## Summary
This paper provides a broad overview of the convergence between IoT and Edge Computing. It emphasizes the shift from centralized cloud architectures to distributed intelligence to solve latency, bandwidth, and security issues.

## Key Analysis
- **Core Focus:** Analysis of energy-efficient hardware, AI-enabled edge nodes, and architectural design models.
- **Emerging Trends:**
    - **Edge AI:** Moving intelligence directly to the node.
    - **Federated Learning:** Privacy-preserving collaborative training.
    - **TinyML:** Implementing ML on ultra-low-power microcontrollers.
- **Challenges Identified:**
    - Interoperability between heterogeneous devices.
    - Energy scarcity in embedded systems.
    - Scaling limitations.
    - Cybersecurity risks at the edge.
- **Relevance:** Useful for high-level trend analysis and identifying "outstanding concerns" in the field of IoT and Edge Computing.
