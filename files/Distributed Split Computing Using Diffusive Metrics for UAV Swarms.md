# Distributed Split Computing Using Diffusive Metrics for UAV Swarms

## Summary
Proposes a fully distributed approach to split computing in large-scale UAV swarms, moving away from centralized orchestration to avoid communication bottlenecks.

## Key Contributions
- **Diffusive Metrics**: Introduces "aggregated gigaflops," an iterative measure of a node's and its neighbors' computing capacity without requiring global network knowledge.
- **Intelligent Forwarding**: Partial inferences are forwarded to underutilized nodes based on local diffusive metrics.
- **Early-Exit Mechanism**: Adapts the inference pathway on-the-fly to handle workload surges.

## Analysis & Results
- **Performance**: Significantly outperforms baseline centralized strategies in terms of latency, fairness, and energy consumption.
- **Scalability**: Designed for large-scale swarms where topology shifts rapidly.
- **Verdict**: Strong contribution to distributed edge intelligence and swarm robotics.
