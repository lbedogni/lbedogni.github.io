# RISE: Relay Inference and Online Scheduling for Efficient Edge-Device Collaborative Diffusion Model Services

**Authors:** Zilan Huang, Zhiqing Tang, Hanshuai Cui, Tian Wang, Yuan Wu, Weijia Jia, Wei Zhao
**Date:** June 2026
**Topic:** Diffusion Models, Edge Computing, Online Scheduling

## Summary
RISE focuses on the deployment of text-to-image diffusion models at the network edge. It introduces a relay inference mechanism and an online scheduling strategy to coordinate the collaborative execution of diffusion models across heterogeneous edge devices, balancing the trade-off between generation quality and latency.

## Analysis
Expands the concept of **Distributed Edge Inference** to generative models other than LLMs (specifically Diffusion Models). The focus on "relay inference" is a specific architectural choice for splitting the iterative denoising process of diffusion models.
