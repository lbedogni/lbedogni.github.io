# FusionSense: Tri-Stage Near-Sensor Learning for Runtime-Adaptive Multimodal Edge Intelligence

**Date:** May 2026
**Keywords:** Multimodal Edge Intelligence, Near-Sensor Learning, Split Computing, Runtime Adaptivity

## Summary
FusionSense proposes a tri-stage architecture that splits computation across near-sensor, edge, and cloud resources. It focuses on runtime adaptivity to meet tight energy, latency, and reliability budgets in autonomous systems and smart-industry deployments. The core challenge addressed is deciding what to compute and transmit at each point of the split for multimodal sensor suites.

## Analysis
This paper advances the state of **Split Computing** by introducing a multi-tier (three-stage) approach rather than a simple binary edge-cloud split. The focus on "near-sensor" learning is particularly relevant for reducing data movement and energy consumption, aligning with the goals of **Distributed Edge Inference**.
