# Lightweight User-Personalization Method for Closed Split Computing (SALT)

**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14958
**Date:** 2026-03-16
**Tags:** #split-computing #personalization #edge-ai #lightweight-tuning

## Summary
The paper introduces **SALT (Split-Adaptive Lightweight Tuning)**, a framework for adapting "closed" Split Computing systems—environments where the underlying model architectures and parameters are inaccessible (frozen). SALT adds a compact client-side adapter that refines intermediate representations before they are sent to the server.

Key achievements:
- **User Personalization**: Improved accuracy from 88.1% to 93.8% on CIFAR-10.
- **Robustness**: Maintained >90% accuracy under 75% packet loss.
- **Efficiency**: Reduced training latency by >60% compared to conventional fine-tuning.

## Analysis
SALT provides a elegant solution for the "closed model" problem in split computing. By focusing on the intermediate representation (the "split point"), it allows for personalization and robustness without needing access to the server-side "tail" or the full client-side "head" weights. This makes it highly viable for commercial deployments where models are provided as black boxes.

## Relevance to Research
Perfect fit for "Split Computing" and "Edge Computing", specifically focusing on the adaptability and robustness of split-inference pipelines.
