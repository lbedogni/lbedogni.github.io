# Agentic IoT: Architectures, Applications, and Challenges Toward the Internet of Agents
**arXiv ID:** 2607.04219
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04219
**Date:** July 2026

## Summary
Presents **Agentic IoT**, a paradigm shift from AIoT (focused on task-specific inference) to an ecosystem of autonomous AI agents. This approach integrates perception, reasoning, planning, and autonomous coordination across the device-edge-fog-cloud continuum, creating an "Internet of Agents."

## Analysis
- **Core Innovation:** Conceptual framework for transitioning from data-centric sensing to cognitive agent ecosystems.
- **Key Themes:**
    - Autonomous coordination.
    - Tool use and contextual decision-making at the edge.
    - Distributed cognitive agent ecosystems.
- **Relevance:** Provides the theoretical and architectural foundation for the next generation of Distributed Edge Inference and IoT, moving from passive inference to active, autonomous agency.
