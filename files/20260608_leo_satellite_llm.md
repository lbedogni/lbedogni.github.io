# Communication-Efficient Collaborative LLM Inference over LEO Satellite Networks

**Date:** 2026-06-08
**Field:** Distributed Inference, LEO Satellites, LLMs

## Summary
The paper proposes a collaborative inference scheme for Large Language Models (LLMs) deployed across Low Earth Orbit (LEO) satellite networks. The LLM is split into sub-models across multiple satellites, which exchange intermediate activations to perform a single inference task.

## Analysis
This extends the concept of Split Computing to the extreme edge (space). The constraints here are extreme: high latency, limited power, and dynamic topology. It demonstrates the versatility of distributed inference beyond terrestrial edge computing and presents a challenging case for communication efficiency.
