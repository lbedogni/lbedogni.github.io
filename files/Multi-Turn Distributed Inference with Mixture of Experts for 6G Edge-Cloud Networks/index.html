Multi-Turn Distributed Inference with Mixture of Experts for 6G Edge-Cloud Networks

Summary

This paper explores the deployment of Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architectures across 6G edge-cloud networks. By leveraging the sparse activation property of MoE, the authors propose a distributed inference scheme that reduces the computational burden on resource-constrained edge nodes without sacrificing the overall model capacity.

Analysis

The use of MoE is a strategic choice for edge computing because it allows for “conditional computation,” where only a subset of the model is active for any given input. Distributing these experts across a 6G continuum allows for a more flexible allocation of resources, potentially reducing latency and energy consumption at the edge. This is highly relevant to the goal of scalable distributed edge inference.
