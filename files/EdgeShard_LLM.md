# EdgeShard: Efficient LLM Inference via Collaborative Edge Computing

**Source**: IEEE Xplore
**URL**: https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10818760
**Date**: 2025/2026

## Summary
EdgeShard focuses on the challenge of deploying Large Language Models (LLMs) on the edge. Instead of relying on the cloud, it proposes a collaborative edge computing approach to distribute LLM inference across multiple edge nodes.

## Key Insights
- Addresses cloud-dependency issues: prolonged latency, high bandwidth costs, and privacy concerns.
- Employs collaborative inference to utilize the aggregate resources of edge devices.

## Analysis & Relevance
Extremely relevant to **Distributed Edge Inference**. The shift towards "Edge LLMs" is a critical frontier in Edge Computing, and collaborative inference (sharding the model across nodes) is a key technique for overcoming the memory/compute limits of individual edge devices.
