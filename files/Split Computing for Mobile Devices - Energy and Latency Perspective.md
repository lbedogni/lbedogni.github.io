# Split Computing for Mobile Devices: Energy and Latency Perspective

## Summary
This paper explores the trade-offs between inference latency and energy consumption in split computing for mobile devices. It highlights the difficulty of optimizing both metrics simultaneously due to the dynamic nature of edge environments.

## Analysis
The focus on simultaneous optimization is critical for real-world mobile deployment where battery life and user experience (latency) are equally important.
