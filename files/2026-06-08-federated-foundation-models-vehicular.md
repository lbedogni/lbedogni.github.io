# Federated Foundation Models over Vehicular Networks
**Authors:** Kasra Borazjani, et al.
**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06786
**Date:** 2026-06-08

## Summary
This work explores integrating multi-modal multi-task federated foundation models (M3T FedFMs) into vehicular networks. It discusses the principles of training and fine-tuning these models in a distributed, privacy-preserving manner to enable "vehicular intelligence."
- **Implementation:** Includes a case study on the Waymo Open Dataset.
- **Contribution:** Identifies key constraints of vehicular environments and proposes research directions for deployment.

## Analysis & Relevance
Relevant to **Edge Computing and Distributed Inference**. Vehicular networks are a prime example of high-mobility edge environments. Using foundation models in a federated manner here is a cutting-edge application of distributed edge inference.
