# Edge-AI: A systematic review on architectures, applications, and...

**Source**: ScienceDirect / Elsevier
**URL**: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1084804525002723
**Date**: 2025

## Summary
A systematic review of the Edge-AI (or Edge Intelligence) field. It analyzes the architectures and applications required to mitigate the limitations of both pure cloud and pure edge executions of deep learning.

## Key Insights
- Highlights the trade-off between the limited resources of edge devices and the latency/bandwidth costs of the cloud.
- Discusses interdisciplinary approaches to optimize DL execution closer to the user.

## Analysis & Relevance
Provides a strong theoretical and architectural foundation for **IoT** and **Edge Computing**. Useful for tracking the current state-of-the-art in Edge-AI architectures.
