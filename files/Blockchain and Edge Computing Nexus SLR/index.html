Blockchain and Edge Computing Nexus: A Large-scale Systematic Literature Review

Summary

This large-scale SLR analyzes the interplay between blockchain and edge computing, focusing on how their decentralized natures can be synergized to solve challenges in Smart Cities, supply chains, and mobility.

Three Perspectives of Interaction

  1. Blockchain-assisted Edge: Using blockchain for security, trust, and data integrity in edge scenarios (e.g., secure data sharing).
  2. Edge-assisted Blockchain: Using edge infrastructure to reduce blockchain latency and improve throughput (e.g., lightweight consensus).
  3. Synergistic Integration: Combined systems for secure edge AI inference, federated learning, and decentralized finance (DeFi).

Analysis

This paper is highly relevant to Distributed Edge Inference from a coordination and trust perspective. While “split computing” usually focuses on the technical partition of a neural network, this paper introduces the governance and incentive layer. The mention of DePIN (Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Networks) suggests a future where distributed inference is performed across a peer-to-peer network of edge devices, where blockchain manages the task allocation and rewards, effectively creating a global, decentralized inference engine.
