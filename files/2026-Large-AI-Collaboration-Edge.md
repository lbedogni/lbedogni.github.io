# Toward Resource-Efficient Collaboration of Large AI Models in Mobile Edge Networks
**Date:** 2026-01-11
**Source:** arXiv (Peichun Li et al.)

## Summary
This research explores the paradigm of collaborating large AI models within mobile edge networks. It focuses on creating resource-efficient frameworks that can distribute the execution of large-scale models across a heterogeneous set of edge nodes to meet the latency and accuracy demands of intelligent services.

## Analysis
As AI models grow in size, the "collaboration" aspect—how models are partitioned and how nodes synchronize—becomes the primary bottleneck. This paper contributes to the architectural understanding of how to scale large models at the edge.

## Key Results
- Framework for resource-efficient collaboration in mobile edge environments.
- Analysis of trade-offs between model partitioning and network overhead.
