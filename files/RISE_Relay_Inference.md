# RISE: Relay Inference and Online Scheduling for Efficient Edge-Device Collaborative Diffusion Model Services

**Date Found:** 2026-06-24
**Source:** arXiv (Submitted 2026-06-15)
**Category:** Edge Computing / Collaborative Inference

## Summary
This paper proposes "RISE", a framework for deploying text-to-image diffusion models at the network edge. It addresses the challenge of serving heterogeneous workloads with varying quality and latency requirements across diverse edge devices.

## Key Contributions
- **Relay Inference:** A mechanism to collaborate between edge devices for diffusion model execution.
- **Online Scheduling:** Dynamic scheduling to optimize for quality and latency.
- **Edge-Device Collaboration:** Specifically targets the deployment of large diffusion models on resource-constrained edge hardware.

## Analysis
This is highly relevant to Luca's interest in **Distributed Edge Inference**. It explores how to split or relay the heavy computation of diffusion models across a network of edge devices, which is a practical application of split computing and distributed inference.
