# SALT: Lightweight User-Personalization Method for Closed Split Computing
**arXiv:** 2603.14958

## Summary
SALT (Split-Adaptive Lightweight Tuning) is a lightweight adaptation framework for "closed" Split Computing systems—where the model architectures and parameters of the head (edge) and tail (server) networks are inaccessible.

## Key Approach
Introduces a compact client-side adapter that refines the intermediate representations produced by the frozen head network. This allows for adaptation without modifying the original networks or increasing communication overhead.

## Results
- **Personalization:** Improved accuracy from 88.1% to 93.8% on CIFAR-10.
- **Efficiency:** Reduced training latency by >60%.
- **Robustness:** Maintained >90% accuracy under 75% packet loss.
- **Privacy:** Preserved high accuracy under noise injection (sigma = 1.0).

## Analysis
SALT solves a major practical hurdle in Split Computing: the ability to personalize or harden a system when you don't own the model weights (closed-box). The use of intermediate representation refinement is an elegant way to inject adaptation without retraining.
