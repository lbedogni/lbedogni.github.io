# SubEdge: A Subscriber-Centric Edge Computing Subsystem in 6G Networks for AI

**Status:** To Read
**Date Found:** 2026-07-01
**Field:** Edge Computing / 6G / Distributed AI

## Abstract
Beyond traditional connectivity, 6G is envisioned to transform mobile networks into a distributed fabric that provides native integrated communication, computing, and intelligence services. AI-native terminals (e.g., robots, autonomous vehicles, and smart glasses) require real-time intelligence. This paper introduces SubEdge, a subscriber-centric edge computing subsystem designed to meet these needs in 6G networks.

## Initial Analysis
This represents the convergence of 6G and Edge AI. The "subscriber-centric" approach suggests a shift in how resources are allocated and how intelligence is distributed across the network fabric. It's a broader architectural view of Distributed Edge Inference.
