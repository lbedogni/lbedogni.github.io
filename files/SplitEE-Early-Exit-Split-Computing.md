# SplitEE: Early Exit in Deep Neural Networks with Split Computing

**Source**: [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3639856.3639873)
**Date**: Dec 2025
**Field**: Split Computing, Early Exit, Edge AI

## Summary
SplitEE is an algorithm that combines two efficiency techniques—**Split Computing** and **Early Exits**—to optimize the deployment of Deep Neural Networks (DNNs) on resource-constrained devices like mobile phones and IoT systems.

## Key Contributions
- **Hybrid Approach**: Integrates split computing (partitioning the model between device and server) with early exits (terminating inference early if a confident prediction is reached).
- **Resource Optimization**: Aims to reduce both the computational load on the edge device and the communication cost to the server.

## Analysis
- This paper is a strong example of combining multiple "edge-native" optimization techniques to maximize efficiency.
- Relevant for research into **Split Computing** and **Distributed Edge Inference**.
