# Adaptive-Frequency Resonate-and-Fire Neurons for Spectral Estimation of Streaming Radar Signals

**Submitted:** June 11, 2026
**Authors:** Stefano Chiavazza, Sen Yuan, Marc Geilen, Francesco Fioranelli, Federico Corradi
**Source:** arXiv

## Summary
This work introduces a neuromorphic-inspired signal processing method based on **adaptive resonate-and-fire neurons** specifically for the spectral estimation of streaming radar signals. The method aims to reduce the memory and latency bottlenecks associated with traditional spectral analysis in low-latency applications.

## Analysis
- **Innovation**: The use of *adaptive* frequency neurons allows the system to tune itself to the signal of interest, providing a more flexible and efficient way to perform spectral estimation at the edge.
- **Relevance to Luca**: Relevant to **IoT** and **Edge Computing**, specifically in the context of real-time sensing and signal processing. It demonstrates a practical application of neuromorphic principles to solve a specific industrial/sensing problem (radar).
- **Potential Impact**: Reduces the computational burden of spectral analysis on edge devices, enabling faster response times for radar-based sensing systems.
