# SubEdge: A Subscriber-Centric Edge Computing Subsystem in 6G Networks for AI

**Date Found:** 2026-07-05
**Source:** arXiv (Submitted 29 June 2026)

## Summary
SubEdge is proposed as a specialized subsystem within 6G networks designed to transform mobile networks into a distributed fabric of communication, computing, and intelligence. It specifically targets AI-native terminals (autonomous vehicles, robotics) that require deterministic, real-time AI services.

## Analysis
Relevant for the **IoT** and **Edge Computing** infrastructure. It highlights the shift toward "AI-native" networks where computing is not just an add-on but a core part of the network fabric (6G), enabling more seamless distributed inference for high-mobility agents.
