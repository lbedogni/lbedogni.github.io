# HyPE: Hybrid Predictive-in-context-lEarning for AI Service Provisioning

**Source:** [arXiv:2607.06278](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06278)
**Date:** July 2026
**Field:** Edge Computing / 6G / AI Service Provisioning

## Summary
HyPE is a framework for quality-aware personalized AI service provisioning in UAV-assisted 6G networks. It focuses on jointly optimizing conventional quality (latency) and Quality of AI Services (QoAIS), such as output fidelity and personalization.

## Key Innovations
- **Predictive Learning:** Uses mobility-aware prediction to forecast request distributions.
- **LLM-based Reasoning:** Leverages LLMs to optimize UAV trajectories and the assignment of inference tasks.
- **Hybrid Approach:** Combines predictive learning with heuristic service placement and routing.

## Results & Analysis
- **Efficiency:** Demonstrates near-optimal coverage and reduced end-to-end latency.
- **Personalization:** Sustains QoAIS-driven service personalization in dynamic aerial-terrestrial settings.
- **Scalability:** Presents a scalable alternative to intractable optimal solutions for joint trajectory and routing problems.

## Relevance to Luca's Research
Relevant to **Edge Computing** and **Distributed Edge Inference**. While not "Split Computing" in the architectural sense, it addresses the high-level orchestration of where inference happens (UAVs vs Ground Edge) and how to maintain quality of service in a highly dynamic distributed environment.
