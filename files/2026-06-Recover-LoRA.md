# Recover-LoRA for Aggressive Quantization: Reclaiming Accuracy in 2-Bit Language Models

**Submitted:** 2 June 2026
**Source:** arXiv

## Summary
Recover-LoRA focuses on reclaiming the accuracy lost during aggressive 2-bit quantization of LLMs. It employs Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) combined with knowledge distillation on synthetic data to refine the quantized weights.

## Analysis
Critical for **On-Device Inference** and **Edge AI**. 2-bit quantization is often necessary to fit LLMs into the limited memory of edge devices, but the resulting accuracy drop is usually prohibitive. Recover-LoRA provides a pathway to maintain high-performance inference on extremely constrained hardware.
