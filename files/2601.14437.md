# Agentic AI Meets Edge Computing in Autonomous UAV Swarms
**arXiv ID:** 2601.14437v1
**Date:** Jan 2026
**Field:** Agentic AI / Edge Computing / UAVs

## Summary
Investigates the integration of LLM-based agentic AI and edge computing to realize scalable and resilient autonomy in UAV swarms.

## Key Contributions
- **Architecture Analysis:** Compares standalone, edge-enabled, and edge-cloud hybrid deployments.
- **SAR Use Case:** Demonstrates that edge-enabled architecture improves search and rescue (SAR) coverage and reduces mission completion times.
- **Challenges:** Identifies open problems in integrating LLMs with mission-critical edge systems.

## Analysis
This paper bridges the gap between the recent trend of "Agentic AI" and the structural constraints of Edge Computing, showing the practical benefits of edge-enabled autonomy.
