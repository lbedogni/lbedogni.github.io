# Quality-Aware Personalized AI Service Provisioning in UAV-Assisted 6G Networks

## Summary
This paper proposes **HyPE (Hybrid Predictive-in-context-lEarning)**, a framework designed to optimize AI service provisioning in 6G networks assisted by Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs). The authors argue that existing methods focus too much on conventional quality (latency) and ignore the **Quality of AI Services (QoAIS)**, which includes output fidelity, continuity, and personalization.

### Key Components of HyPE:
1. **Mobility-Aware Prediction**: Forecasts spatio-temporal request distributions.
2. **Learning-Augmented Decision**: Uses LLM-based reasoning to optimize UAV trajectories and inference assignments.
3. **Heuristic-Based Placement**: Handles service placement and routing.

## Analysis & Results
The framework addresses the challenge of maintaining high QoAIS in dynamic aerial-terrestrial settings. Simulations show that HyPE achieves:
- Near-optimal coverage.
- Reduced end-to-end latency.
- Sustained service personalization and continuity compared to state-of-the-art baselines.

## Relevance to Luca's Research
Directly relevant to **Edge Computing** and **Distributed Inference**, specifically in the context of 6G and aerial edge nodes. The use of LLM-based reasoning for resource orchestration (trajectories and assignments) is a modern approach to the "inference assignment" problem in distributed systems.
