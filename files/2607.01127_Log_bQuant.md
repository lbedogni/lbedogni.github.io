# Log_bQuant: Quantizing Language Models in Logarithmic Space
**arXiv ID:** 2607.01127v1
**Date:** 2026-07-01

## Summary
This paper introduces **Log_bQuant**, a novel logarithmic quantization approach for Large Language Models (LLMs) with adjustable bases. Unlike traditional uniform quantization, which often fails to represent low-frequency high-magnitude weights efficiently, Log_bQuant adapts to the common parameter distributions of LLMs.

## Analysis
The authors demonstrate that using a logarithmic scale for quantization codebooks allows for better representation of weights that span several orders of magnitude. This is particularly critical for maintaining accuracy at very low bit-widths (e.g., 4-bit).

## Key Results
- **Precision:** Outperforms asymmetric linear quantization at 4-bit precision across several benchmarks.
- **Efficiency:** Achieves significant memory savings and moderate inference speedups.
- **Deployment:** Specifically targets the feasibility of running private LLMs on consumer-grade GPUs and edge devices.
