# CADET: A Modular Platform for Evaluating Distributed Cooperative Autonomy in Connected Autonomous Vehicles

**Date:** 2026-06-02
**Source:** arXiv
**Tags:** #DistributedInference #AutonomousVehicles #EdgeComputing #FoundationModels

## Summary
CADET is a modular platform designed to evaluate distributed cooperative autonomy in Connected Autonomous Vehicles (CAVs). The paper highlights that while distributed perception and control can improve safety and efficiency, they introduce critical system-level challenges:
- **Network Latency:** Real-time decision-making is hindered by communication delays.
- **Compute Heterogeneity:** Different vehicles/edge nodes have varying capabilities.
- **Multi-tenant Contention:** Resource competition among multiple services.
- **Foundation Model Scale:** The increasing reliance on large foundation models necessitates cloud/edge deployment, amplifying these challenges.

## Analysis & Relevance

**Key Takeaway:** A systematic approach to quantifying the impact of network and compute bottlenecks on cooperative autonomy.
