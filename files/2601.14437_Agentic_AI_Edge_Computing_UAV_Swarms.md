# Agentic AI Meets Edge Computing in Autonomous UAV Swarms (arXiv:2601.14437v1)

## Summary
This research explores the synergy between **Agentic AI** (LLMs with autonomous reasoning/planning) and **Edge Computing** for UAV swarms. It compares three deployment architectures: Standalone, Edge-enabled, and Edge-Cloud Hybrid, using a wildfire Search and Rescue (SAR) scenario as a benchmark.

## Analysis
This represents the next evolution of **Distributed Edge Inference**: moving from simple model partitioning (Split Computing) to deploying autonomous agents at the edge. It demonstrates how edge-enabled reasoning can significantly improve mission efficiency in high-risk, dynamic environments.

## Key Takeaways
- **Architecture**: The "Edge-enabled" architecture is identified as optimal for balancing autonomy and connectivity.
- **Impact**: Reduced mission completion times and increased search coverage in SAR operations.
- **Trend**: Integration of LLMs into the edge-robotic loop for real-time mission-critical autonomy.
