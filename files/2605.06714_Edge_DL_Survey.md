# Edge Deep Learning in Computer Vision and Medical Diagnostics: A Comprehensive Survey
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.06714
- **Date:** May 2026
- **Topic:** Edge AI, Deep Learning, Survey, Medical Diagnostics

## Summary
A comprehensive review of the state-of-the-art in Edge Deep Learning, focusing on computer vision and medical diagnostics. It covers foundational principles, a new categorization of edge hardware platforms, and methods for lightweight DNN implementation.

## Key Results
- Provides a structured taxonomy of edge hardware.
- Reviews current model compression and lightweight design techniques.
- Analyzes obstacles to widespread adoption in medical fields.

## Analysis
A great "big picture" reference for Luca. Useful for keeping up with the general trends and hardware landscape in the Edge AI domain.
