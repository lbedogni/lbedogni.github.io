# From Sensors to Data Intelligence: Leveraging IoT, Cloud, and Edge Computing with AI
**Source:** MDPI (https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/25/6/1763)
**Date Found:** 2026-07-01

## Summary
This work analyzes integration strategies among IoT, cloud, and edge computing combined with AI to transform raw sensor data into "actionable intelligence" for pervasive environmental monitoring.

## Key Concepts
- **Tiered Architecture:** Analyzes the flow of data from Sensors $\rightarrow$ Edge $\rightarrow$ Cloud, optimizing where the AI inference happens based on urgency and resource availability.
- **Pervasive Intelligence:** Focuses on seamless integration to achieve intelligence in smart cities and healthcare.
- **Integration Approaches:** Compares different ways of splitting the intelligence load between the cloud and the edge.

## Analysis for Luca
Provides a broader architectural view of **Edge Computing** and **IoT**. While less technical on the "split" mechanism than the other papers, it provides a good framework for the "where and why" of distributed inference.
