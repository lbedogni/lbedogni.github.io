# IoT-Enhanced Virtual Power Plants with Edge Computing and Blockchain Security for Sustainable Smart Grid Management

- **Source:** Nature ([Link](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-47217-y))
- **Date:** 2026
- **Tags:** #IoT #EdgeComputing #Blockchain #SmartGrid #VPP

## Summary
The paper proposes a unified architecture integrating **IoT, hybrid edge-fog computing, DPoS-PBFT blockchain, and Reinforcement Learning (RL)** to manage Virtual Power Plants (VPPs) more efficiently. It addresses the limitations of centralized cloud control (high latency) and cybersecurity vulnerabilities in smart grids.

## Key Contributions & Results
- **Latency:** Achieved sub-50ms end-to-end response latency (a 92.7% reduction over cloud-only systems) using a hybrid edge-fog workload partitioning strategy.
- **Security:** Implemented a DPoS-PBFT consensus mechanism specifically for IoT, reducing blockchain consensus latency to 3.8ms.
- **Economics:** RL-based market bidding and personalized demand response increased revenue by 18.4% and user participation by 116.8%.
- **Efficiency:** 23.1% improvement in energy utilization efficiency and a 25% increase in renewable energy utilization through intelligent V2G coordination.
- **Validation:** Validated using the Pecan Street real-world dataset with scalability demonstrated up to 10,000+ DER nodes.
