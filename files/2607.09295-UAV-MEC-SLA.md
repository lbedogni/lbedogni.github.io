# Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning for SLA-Aware Network Slicing in UAV-Enabled MEC

**arXiv ID:** 2607.09295
**Date:** July 2026
**Category:** Edge Computing, UAVs, MARL

## Summary
This paper addresses the challenge of guaranteeing slice-level Service-Level Agreements (SLAs) in UAV-enabled Mobile Edge Computing (MEC). The environment is characterized by dynamic user mobility, stochastic task arrivals, and limited onboard energy and computing resources.

## Key Contributions
- **Predictive MARL Framework**: Proposes a framework that coordinates UAV trajectories and computation resource allocation to maintain SLA stability.
- **Mobility Prediction**: Implements a lightweight module to forecast near-future user mobility, allowing UAVs to reposition proactively.
- **SLA-Aware Reward Function**: The reward function explicitly penalizes SLA violation probability, duration, and energy consumption.
- **Training**: Utilizes Multi-Agent Proximal Policy Optimization (MAPPO) with centralized training and decentralized execution (CTDE).

## Results & Analysis
- Significantly improves SLA stability compared to baselines.
- Maintains competitive energy efficiency and delay performance.
- Performance approaches "oracle-level" results when predictive information is accurate.

## Relevance to Research
Relevant for studies on **Edge Computing** and **IoT** orchestration, specifically in scenarios where infrastructure is mobile (UAVs) and resource constraints are severe.
