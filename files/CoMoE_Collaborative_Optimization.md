# CoMoE: Collaborative Optimization of Expert Aggregation and Offloading for MoE-based LLMs at Edge

- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.09208
- **Topic**: Distributed Edge Inference, MoE LLMs
- **Summary**: This paper proposes CoMoE, a framework for the collaborative optimization of expert aggregation and offloading specifically for Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) based Large Language Models (LLMs) deployed at the edge. By optimizing how experts are aggregated and offloaded between edge devices and servers, it aims to reduce latency and resource consumption while maintaining model performance.
- **Analysis**: Highly relevant to Luca's interest in Distributed Edge Inference. The use of MoE allows for more flexible splitting of the model across the network, making it a prime candidate for split computing research.
