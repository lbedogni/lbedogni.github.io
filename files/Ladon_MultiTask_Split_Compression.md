# Ladon: A Multi-task Supervised Compression Model for Split Computing

- **URL:** [https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.01420](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.01420)
- **Date:** January 2025
- **Field:** Split Computing / Model Compression

## Summary
Ladon introduces the first multi-task-head supervised compression model designed for **multi-task split computing**. It focuses on learning compressed representations at the early layers of the DNN to reduce the amount of data transmitted from the sensor/mobile device to the edge server.

## Analysis
Directly relevant to **Split Computing**. The impressive results (up to 95.4% reduction in latency and 88.2% reduction in energy) highlight the importance of the "bottleneck" at the split point. For Luca, this provides a concrete example of how to handle multi-task scenarios in split computing without sacrificing too much accuracy.
