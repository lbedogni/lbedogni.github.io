# Spatio-Temporal Scheduling Prediction Under Backhaul Delay for Resilient Coordinated Beamforming

**arXiv ID:** 2607.08454
**Date:** July 2026
**Category:** Distributed Networking, 5G, GNNs

## Summary
The paper focuses on the problem of stale scheduling information in distributed 5G networks due to backhaul latency, which degrades the performance of Coordinated Beamforming (CBF).

## Key Contributions
- **StemGNN**: Introduces a Spectral Temporal Graph Neural Network (StemGNN) to predict future User Equipment (UE) scheduling states based on delayed historical data.
- **Predictive Framework**: A two-stage framework where StemGNN predictions replace stale inputs in the CBF-SLNR precoder.
- **Performance**: Achieves a mean scheduling prediction accuracy of 87.57%.

## Results & Analysis
- Recovers 57-73% of the sum rate loss caused by a single TTI of backhaul delay.
- Improves sum rate by 9.58-14.35% over the no-prediction baseline.
- Recovers up to 83% of fairness loss for cell-edge users.
- Demonstrates that spatio-temporal forecasting can mitigate the impact of backhaul latency in coordinated networks.

## Relevance to Research
Directly applicable to **Distributed Edge Inference** and **Distributed Systems** where communication delays between nodes (backhaul) impact the coherence and performance of coordinated tasks.
