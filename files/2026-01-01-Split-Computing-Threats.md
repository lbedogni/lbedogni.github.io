# AI-Based Detection and Classification of Adversarial and Fault-Induced Threats in Split Computing

**Date:** 2026-01-01
**Authors:** G. Esposito et al.
**Source:** ResearchGate (Limited Access)
**Field:** Split Computing, Security, AI/ML

## Summary
This paper focuses on the security and reliability of Split Computing architectures. It proposes an AI-based approach to detect and classify threats that are either adversarial (intentional attacks) or fault-induced (hardware/software failures) within the split computing pipeline.

## Analysis (Based on Available Metadata)
Split computing introduces a unique attack surface at the "split point" where intermediate tensors are transmitted. This paper likely addresses the vulnerability of these tensors to manipulation or corruption. By classifying these threats, the system can potentially trigger recovery mechanisms or alert the operators to a security breach.
