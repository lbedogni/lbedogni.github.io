# FusionSense

**Summary:**
FusionSense proposes a tri-stage computation split across **near-sensor, edge, and cloud** resources. It emphasizes runtime adaptivity to meet tight energy and latency budgets, dynamically deciding which parts of a multimodal sensor suite's computation should happen at each stage.

**Analysis:**
Fits perfectly into **Edge Computing** and **IoT**. The "near-sensor" aspect is particularly interesting as it pushes the "edge" even further toward the hardware, reducing data movement before it even reaches an edge gateway.
