# Energy-Aware Computing in the Year 2026 (arXiv:2605.24569v1)

## Summary
A comprehensive survey of energy-efficient computing across the **cloud-edge-HPC continuum**. It focuses on the systemic bottleneck of energy consumption in the era of Exascale computing and generative AI, proposing a taxonomy for green computing including hardware/software optimizations, dynamic scheduling, and Federated Learning.

## Analysis
While a survey, this provides the necessary "big picture" for Luca's research. It bridges **Edge Computing** with larger-scale HPC and sustainability, highlighting the necessity of energy-aware distributed inference in the current AI landscape.

## Key Takeaways
- **Holistic View**: Covers everything from IoT microcontrollers to multi-megawatt data centers.
- **AI Focus**: Explicitly analyzes the resource requirements of large-scale AI and the path toward sustainable computing.
- **Roadmap**: Offers a forward-looking perspective on the "Green ICT" regulation and technical trends.
