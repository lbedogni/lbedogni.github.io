# [2505.01821] Edge-Cloud Collaborative Computing on Distributed Intelligence and Model Optimization: A Survey

**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.01821
**Date:** May 2025 (v5 Mar 2026)
**Authors:** Jing Liu, Yao Du, Kun Yang, et al.

## Summary
A comprehensive survey on Edge-Cloud Collaborative Computing (ECCC), focusing on the synergy between distributed intelligence and model optimization. It explores architectures and technologies that allow LLMs and deep learning models to be deployed efficiently across edge-cloud environments.

## Key Analysis
- **Model Optimization:** Discusses compression, adaptation, and Neural Architecture Search (NAS) to balance performance, energy, and latency.
- **Resource Management:** Focuses on AI-driven strategies for handling heterogeneity in edge devices.
- **Applications:** Highlights critical use cases in autonomous driving, healthcare, and industrial automation.
- **Future Directions:** Identifies LLM deployment, 6G integration, neuromorphic computing, and quantum computing as the next frontiers.

## Relevance to Luca
Directly aligns with research interests in **Edge Computing** and **Distributed Edge Inference**. Provides a holistic roadmap and benchmarking standards for these systems.
