# Edge-Cloud Collaborative Computing on Distributed Intelligence and Model Optimization: A Survey

**Source:** [arXiv:2505.01821](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.01821) | IEEE ComST
**Date:** March 2026 (v5)
**Keywords:** #IoT #EdgeComputing #DistributedIntelligence #ModelOptimization #LLM #6G

## Summary
A comprehensive 45-page survey on Edge-Cloud Collaborative Computing (ECCC). It examines the intersection of distributed intelligence and model optimization, providing a structured tutorial on:
- **Fundamental Architectures:** How cloud and edge resources are integrated for low-latency processing.
- **Model Optimization:** Detailed analysis of compression, adaptation, and Neural Architecture Search (NAS).
- **Resource Management:** AI-driven strategies to balance performance, energy, and latency.
- **Security & Privacy:** Protection mechanisms within distributed ECCC systems.
- **Practical Applications:** Autonomous driving, healthcare, and industrial automation.

## Analysis
This paper serves as a state-of-the-art roadmap for the field. It's particularly valuable for its discussion on **LLM deployment at the edge** and the integration with **6G**, neuromorphic computing, and quantum computing. For Luca's research, it provides the necessary theoretical and architectural context for Distributed Edge Inference.
