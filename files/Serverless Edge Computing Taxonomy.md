# Serverless Edge Computing: A Taxonomy and Review

## Summary
A systematic literature review exploring the current landscape of Serverless Edge Computing, focusing on how this paradigm meets critical QoS requirements for the expanding IoT ecosystem.

## Key Focus Areas
- **Architectural Designs**: Analysis of how serverless functions are deployed and orchestrated at the edge.
- **QoS Metrics**: Emphasis on reduced latency, efficient bandwidth usage, scalability, privacy, and security.
- **Taxonomy**: Proposes a comprehensive categorization of existing research efforts to provide a structured view of the field.

## Analysis
This paper serves as a foundational map for the "Serverless at the Edge" trend. It highlights the shift from traditional VM/Container-based edge deployments to event-driven, fine-grained function execution, which is essential for the bursty and diverse nature of IoT workloads.

## Open Challenges
The review identifies significant gaps in current research regarding the coordination of stateful functions at the edge and the optimization of cold-start latencies in highly distributed environments.
