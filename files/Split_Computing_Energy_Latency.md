# Split Computing for Mobile Devices: Energy and Latency Perspective

- **Source**: IEEE Xplore (2026)
- **Field**: Split Computing / Mobile Edge
- **Status**: #to-read

## Summary
This research provides a comprehensive perspective on the trade-offs between **inference latency** and **energy consumption** in split computing for mobile devices. It argues that optimizing one often degrades the other, and proposes strategies to balance both.

## Analysis
While many papers focus purely on speed (latency), this work emphasizes the "energy" side of the equation, which is the primary constraint for mobile/battery-powered IoT devices. It provides a framework for understanding how split points and communication overhead impact the device's battery life.

## Relevance
Crucial for any practical implementation of Split Computing in IoT/Mobile environments where energy efficiency is as important as real-time performance.
