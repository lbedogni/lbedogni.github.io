# Neuromorphic Wireless Split Computing with Resonate-and-Fire Neurons
- **Date**: June 14, 2026
- **Source**: arXiv
- **Topic**: Neuromorphic Computing, Wireless Split Computing, RF Neurons

## Summary
The researchers explore a wireless split computing architecture that utilizes **Resonate-and-Fire (RF) neurons** to process time-domain signals directly. This approach eliminates traditional preprocessing steps by leveraging the oscillatory dynamics of RF neurons, which can better capture rich spectral features.

## Analysis
Integrating neuromorphic (SNN) principles into split computing is a significant leap for energy efficiency. By using RF neurons, the system can handle complex time-series data (like audio or vibration) at the edge with minimal power, transmitting only sparse spikes to the server, thus optimizing the communication-computation trade-off.
