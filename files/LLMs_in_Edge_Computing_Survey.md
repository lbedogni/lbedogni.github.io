# LLMs in Edge Computing: Applications, Challenges, and Future Directions
**Source:** [Frontiers](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/computer-science/articles/10.3389/fcomp.2025.1538277/full)
**Date:** 2025
**Tags:** #LLM #EdgeComputing #ModelCompression #DistributedLearning

## Summary
This survey explores the intersection of Large Language Models (LLMs) and edge computing, focusing on adapting resource-heavy models (like GPT-4, BERT) for constrained edge environments.

### Key Themes:
- **Challenges**: Limited computational power, energy efficiency, and network reliability on edge devices.
- **Optimization Strategies**: 
    - **Model Compression**: Pruning, quantization, and knowledge distillation.
    - **Distributed Learning**: Using federated learning to train/update models without moving raw data.
    - **Edge-Friendly Architectures**: Designing models specifically for local execution.
- **Applications**: Healthcare, smart cities, and IoT-based intelligent data analysis.

## Analysis
The paper highlights the inevitable tension between the scale of LLMs and the constraints of the edge. The focus on "distributed learning" and "compression" mirrors the core goals of Split Computing and Distributed Inference—moving the compute to where the data is, or splitting the model to fit the hardware. It provides a roadmap for how generative AI can be decentralized.
