# CANS: Accelerating Multiuser Collaborative Edge Inference via Cooperative Autodidactic NeuroSurgeon

**Authors:** Zheshun Wu, Ziyang Zhang, Changyao Lin, Zenglin Xu, Jie Liu
**Date:** June 2026
**Topic:** MEC, Collaborative DNN Inference, Edge AI

## Summary
CANS proposes a framework to accelerate collaborative Deep Neural Network (DNN) inference in Mobile Edge Computing (MEC) environments. It utilizes a "Cooperative Autodidactic NeuroSurgeon" to optimize the split points and the distribution of the model across the edge-cloud continuum, specifically for multiuser scenarios where resource contention is high.

## Analysis
Relevant to **Split Computing** and the optimization of **Distributed Edge Inference**. The "autodidactic" (self-learning) nature of the optimization suggests a dynamic adaptation to network conditions, which is crucial for real-world IoT/Edge systems.
