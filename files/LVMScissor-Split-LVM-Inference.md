# LVMScissor: Split and Schedule Large Vision Model Inference on Mobile Edges via Salp Swarm Algorithm

**Source:** IEEE Transactions on Mobile Computing
**Field:** Split Computing, Distributed Edge Inference, Large Vision Models (LVMs)

## Summary
LVMScissor proposes a method to split and schedule the inference of Large Vision Models (LVMs) across mobile edge devices. The core contribution is the use of the Salp Swarm Algorithm to optimize the splitting points and the scheduling of these tasks to maximize efficiency on resource-constrained mobile edges.

## Analysis
This paper is highly relevant to Luca's research in Split Computing and Distributed Edge Inference. While many split computing papers focus on CNNs, the extension to LVMs (which are significantly larger and more complex) is a critical step for deploying advanced AI on mobile devices. The use of a swarm-based optimization algorithm for scheduling suggests a focus on dynamic environments where optimal split points may change based on network conditions or device load.

**Key Takeaways:**
- Targets LVMs specifically.
- Combines model splitting with intelligent scheduling.
- Uses nature-inspired optimization (Salp Swarm) for resource allocation.
