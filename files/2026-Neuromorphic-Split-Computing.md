# Neuromorphic Wireless Split Computing with Resonate-and-Fire Neurons

**Date:** June 2026
**Keywords:** Neuromorphic Computing, Wireless Split Computing, Resonate-and-Fire Neurons, SNN

## Summary
This research explores a wireless split computing architecture employing resonate-and-fire (RF) neurons. Unlike conventional leaky integrate-and-fire (LIF) neurons, RF neurons use oscillatory dynamics to process time-domain signals directly, eliminating the need for complex pre-processing stages and reducing latency.

## Analysis
This work intersects **Neuromorphic Computing** and **Split Computing**. By leveraging the inherent dynamics of RF neurons, it potentially reduces the computational overhead at the edge device before transmission to the server, which is a key bottleneck in split computing.
