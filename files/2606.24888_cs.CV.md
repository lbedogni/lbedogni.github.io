# DiffusionBench: On Holistic Evaluation of Diffusion Transformers
- **Source:** [arXiv:2606.24888](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24888)
- **Date:** 2026-06-23
- **Field:** cs.CV

## Summary
Diffusion transformer (DiT) research on image generation has converged to a single evaluation setup: class-conditional generation on ImageNet. While methods improve the FID and related metrics, it is increasingly unclear whether they reflect real progress in generative modeling. The natural alternative, i.e., text-to-image (T2I) generation, is perceived as too costly or inconvenient to train and evaluate and is often skipped.
