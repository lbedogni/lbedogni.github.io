# 2026 Edge AI Technology Report: Trends, Signals & Strategic Insights

## Summary
A comprehensive report by MAPEGY and Wevolver analyzing the transition from centralized cloud AI to distributed edge intelligence. It maps the technological, economic, and regulatory forces shaping the Edge AI landscape for 2026 and beyond.

## Key Strategic Insights
- **Architectural Shift**: Training remains in the cloud, but inference is aggressively migrating to the edge.
- **The "Edge AI Stack"**:
    - **Models**: Shift toward Small Language Models (SLMs) and heavy use of quantization/distillation.
    - **Hardware**: Rise of NPUs and ultra-low-power neuromorphic computing/TinyML.
    - **Capabilities**: Emergence of Agentic AI (autonomous local agents) and Embodied AI (robotics/spatial perception).
    - **Collaboration**: Move toward Federated Learning and Swarm Intelligence.
- **Regulatory Drivers**: The EU AI Act and Cyber Resilience Act (August/September 2026) are forcing localized data processing for compliance and security.
- **Energy Arbitrage**: Massive energy efficiency gains by moving from watt-scale cloud pipelines to milliwatt-scale edge accelerators.

## Analysis & Relevance
This report provides the "big picture" for Luca's research. It validates the importance of **Distributed Edge Inference** not just as a technical challenge, but as a regulatory and economic necessity. The mention of "Swarm Learning" and "On-device RAG" indicates the next frontiers in the field.
