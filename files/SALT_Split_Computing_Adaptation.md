# Research Paper: SALT

- **Title:** Lightweight User-Personalization Method for Closed Split Computing
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14958
- **Summary:** The paper proposes SALT (Split-Adaptive Lightweight Tuning), a framework for adapting split computing models in "closed" (black-box) environments where architecture and parameters are inaccessible. It uses a compact client-side adapter to refine intermediate representations from a frozen head network.
- **Analysis:** This addresses a critical gap in split computing: adaptation when the model itself cannot be modified. The results show significant improvements in accuracy and efficiency (e.g., higher personalized accuracy and reduced training latency).
