# GA-MO: Pareto-Optimal Split Computing for Deep Edge Intelligence

**Source:** Academia.edu / Research Paper
**Date:** 2026
**Topic:** Split Computing, Deep Edge Intelligence

## Summary
GA-MO focuses on the optimization of "split points" in neural networks for edge intelligence. It seeks to find the optimal balance between local computation (on the device) and remote computation (on the edge server).

## Key Analysis
- **Methodology:** Uses a Genetic Algorithm (GA) and Multi-Objective (MO) optimization to construct **Pareto fronts**.
- **Optimization Parameters:**
    - Split points (where the model is divided).
    - Quantization levels (precision of weights/activations).
    - Compression ratios (reducing data transmission size).
- **Outcome:** Provides a spectrum of latency-bandwidth trade-offs, allowing the system to choose the best operating point based on current network conditions and application requirements.
- **Relevance:** Directly aligns with Luca's interest in Split Computing, providing a mathematical framework for optimizing the split.
