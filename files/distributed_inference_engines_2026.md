# Distributed Inference Engines: Orchestrating Decentralized Small Language Model Clusters

This paper discusses the orchestration of decentralized clusters of small LLMs across edge gateways, micro-servers, and smartphones. It positions these as a collaborative inference fabric to handle edge intelligence effectively.

## Key Takeaways
- Collaborative inference fabric.
- Heterogeneous environment support (gateways, servers, smartphones).
- Addresses performance degradation in isolated edge devices.

## Relevance
Highly relevant to Distributed Edge Inference and IoT edge applications.
