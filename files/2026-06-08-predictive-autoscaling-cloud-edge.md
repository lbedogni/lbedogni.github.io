# Predictive Autoscaling in Cloud-Native and Federated Cloud-Edge Computing Environments: A Taxonomy and Future Directions
**Authors:** Bablu Kumar, Anshul Verma, Rajkumar Buyya
**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07046
**Date:** 2026-06-08

## Summary
This paper provides a structured review of predictive autoscaling in cloud-native and federated cloud-edge environments. It addresses the limitations of traditional reactive autoscaling (fixed thresholds) which often lead to resource imbalance and performance degradation. The authors introduce a taxonomy of autoscaling techniques based on triggers, targets, prediction models, and evaluation metrics. Key areas covered include:
- Kubernetes Custom Resource Definitions (CRDs) and operators.
- Monitor-Analyse-Plan-Execute (MAPE) based control loops.
- Federated Learning (FL) for proactive autoscaling.
- Drift-aware and uncertainty-aware strategies (e.g., Autoscaling Drift Index).

## Analysis & Relevance
Highly relevant to Luca's work on **Edge Computing**. This paper provides a comprehensive map of how to move from reactive to proactive resource management at the edge, which is critical for latency-sensitive applications in distributed environments. The focus on federated environments aligns with the goal of distributed edge inference.
