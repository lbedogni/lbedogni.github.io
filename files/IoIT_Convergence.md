# Internet of Intelligent Things: Convergence of Embedded Systems, Edge Computing, and ML
**URL:** https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2542660524000945
**Date Found:** 2026-07-11

## Summary
The paper defines the "Internet of Intelligent Things" (IoIT) as the convergence point where IoT devices are integrated with embedded AI algorithms, moving beyond simple connectivity to autonomous intelligence at the edge.

## Analysis & Relevance
Provides a conceptual framework for the evolution of IoT toward intelligent edge systems. Relevant for the broader context of Luca's research into Edge Computing.
