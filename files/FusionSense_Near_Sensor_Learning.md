# FusionSense: Tri-Stage Near-Sensor Learning for Runtime-Adaptive Multimodal Edge Intelligence
**Date:** May 19, 2026
**Source:** arXiv

## Summary
FusionSense proposes a **tri-stage computation split** across near-sensor, edge, and cloud resources. It focuses on run-time adaptivity to manage tight energy, latency, and reliability budgets, specifically for multimodal sensor suites.

## Analysis
This paper evolves the traditional binary split (edge/cloud) into a three-tier hierarchy. By introducing "near-sensor" learning, it minimizes data movement at the earliest possible stage, which is critical for ultra-low latency and energy-constrained multimodal AIoT systems.
