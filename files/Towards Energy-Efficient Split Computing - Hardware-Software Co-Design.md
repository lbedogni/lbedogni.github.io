# Towards Energy-Efficient Split Computing: A Hardware-Software Co-Design

## Summary
This work presents a two-phase framework for energy-efficient split computing. It combines offline optimization (to find optimal split points) with dynamic scheduling (to adjust settings at runtime) to balance energy use and latency.

## Analysis
The co-design approach is superior to software-only solutions because it can optimize the actual hardware power states and clock speeds alongside the network partition point.
