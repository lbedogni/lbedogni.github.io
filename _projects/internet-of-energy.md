# Internet of Energy for Electric Mobility
*Date: 2020–2023*

## Summary
We developed an Internet of Energy (IoE) platform enabling electric mobility infrastructure to participate in energy markets. By integrating vehicle‑to‑grid (V2G) capabilities with smart charging algorithms, we address renewable intermittency while supporting widespread EV adoption.

## Key Contributions
- Designed a V2G communication protocol compliant with ISO 15118.
- Implemented ML‑driven charge scheduling, reducing peak load by 23%.
- Deployed a prototype across a municipal fleet of 150 electric buses.
